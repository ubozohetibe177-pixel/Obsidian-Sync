Obsidian-Sync

Obsidian-Sync is a personal-use Google OAuth client. Its owner uses it to sync their own Obsidian Markdown notes between their own devices and their own Google Drive, through the third-party open-source Obsidian plugin GSync.

What it does

  • Lets the GSync plugin, running on the owner's devices, sign in to the owner's Google account.
  • Uses the Google Drive API to list, download, upload and move to trash files in the one Drive folder the owner configured for their notes.

What it is not

This is not a public service. There is no account sign-up, no server and no other users. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, Obsidian or the GSync developer.
